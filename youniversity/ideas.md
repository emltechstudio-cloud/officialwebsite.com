# EML Youniversity Design Direction

## Observed EML brand cues

The current EML Tech Studio site uses a **deep violet-to-electric-cyan vertical gradient rail**, white working surfaces, compact logo placement, bright cyan edging, dark indigo information panels, and occasional warm-yellow calls to action. The interface is asymmetric: the navigation rail remains visually strong while content sits in open white space.

## Three directions considered

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| Signal Classroom | A structured learning environment that grows directly from EML's violet/cyan rail, translating the studio identity into calm, practical study surfaces. | 0.06 |
| Studio Workbook | A light editorial workspace with worksheet-like evidence cards, using EML colour only as a navigation and action system. | 0.04 |
| Launch Pad | A bolder, campaign-led landing sequence that treats each discipline as a bright launch track. | 0.08 |

## Chosen direction: Signal Classroom

**Design Movement.** Digital studio signage combined with a practical learning workbook. The design inherits the EML Tech Studio gradient rail rather than adding a separate education brand.

**Core Principles.** The platform uses a strong left-side signal rail, an airy white work surface, clear task-oriented hierarchy, and coloured states only where they communicate progress or action. It avoids generic centred cards and avoids graduation imagery because Youniversity records self-study rather than formal qualifications.

**Color Philosophy.** Violet communicates the EML studio's technical foundation; cyan is used for learning momentum and active states; midnight indigo holds high-focus information; warm yellow marks a deliberate learner action. White preserves mental space for self-paced work.

**Layout Paradigm.** A persistent asymmetric navigation rail anchors desktop layouts. The main content uses offset editorial columns, progress strips, and variable-sized cards instead of one centred grid. On narrow screens, the rail becomes a compact top signal band.

**Signature Elements.** A thin cyan signal line, rounded gradient discipline tags, and evidence cards with a small “saved work” marker recur across the experience.

**Interaction Philosophy.** Actions state their consequence: enrolling changes a course state, marking a section complete saves progress, and certificates remain unavailable until the backend confirms eligibility. Motion is short, responsive, and never hides system state.

**Animation.** Cards lift by two pixels on hover; panels enter with a 180ms opacity/translate transition; reduced-motion users receive instant state changes. No looping decorative animation is used.

**Typography System.** Sora handles headings and utility labels; DM Sans handles instructional copy and controls. Headlines are compact and directive; body copy is conversational, specific, and readable.

**Brand Essence.** EML Youniversity is a free, practical self-study starting point for learners who want to make a first independent digital project. **Practical, encouraging, accountable.**

**Brand Voice.** Headlines are direct and build-oriented; CTAs describe the next action; microcopy distinguishes self-study records from formal qualifications. Examples: “Make your first working piece.” and “Save your Learner ID before you leave this screen.”

**Wordmark & Logo.** Reuse the existing EML Tech Studio logo as the parent mark, paired with a compact “Youniversity” wordmark and cyan signal dot rather than a default text-only treatment.

**Signature Brand Color.** Signal Cyan — `#17D9E6`.

## Style Decisions

- Use the existing EML logo from the public EML site; do not invent a replacement mark.
- Do not imply credits, degrees, accreditation, professional certification, or guaranteed employment.
- The Learner ID must be visible to the authenticated learner and never be sent by email.
