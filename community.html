Good to know

Questions we are answering openly.

Is this a paid opportunity?

No. This page is for community interest, learning and future participation. There is currently no promised salary, commission or payment structure.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Technology ecosystems need creative, organised, communicative and curious people as well as developers.

Will members get a certificate?

We are exploring credible ways to recognise participation in the future. Any certificate or record will be described clearly and will not be presented as academic accreditation unless it is formally accredited.

What happens after I join?

We will share relevant updates as the community takes shape. Joining the waitlist does not guarantee acceptance into a future programme or activity.
