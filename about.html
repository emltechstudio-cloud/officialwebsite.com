About EML Tech Studio · ETS

A studio for useful digital progress.

EML Tech Studio builds digital experiences and learning spaces for people who want technology to work in the real world.

Who we are

ETS means EML Tech Studio.

You may see the shorter name ETS across our products and community spaces. The full name remains EML Tech Studio: the studio behind our services, tools, education and collaborations.

We work across design, development, education and communication because a useful product is more than code. It needs clear language, thoughtful structure and a path for people to keep using it.

  • PeaceTechnology should create more room for people to think, learn and participate.
  • EducationWe make practical learning and useful documentation part of the work.
  • CommunityPeople can contribute to an ecosystem without having to be expert coders.

Questions we answer

What does ETS actually do?

Is ETS a software company?

It is a digital studio and product ecosystem. We deliver client services while also building our own tools and learning platforms.

Is EML Youniversity a certified university?

It is a practical self-study platform. Its completion records are not degrees, academic credits, licences or accredited qualifications.

Can non-coders join the community?

Yes. The community is for people interested in technology, creativity, communication, learning and digital work—not only programming.
