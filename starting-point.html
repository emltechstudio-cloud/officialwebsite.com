GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

More event information will be shared here as the Premiere details are finalized.

How do I register?

Use any “Register” button on this page. It opens the EML Tech Studio Google Form; complete that form to register your interest in the event.

Where will I watch the sessions?

The event is virtual and is planned as a series of YouTube Premieres. The Premiere links will be shared by EML Tech Studio as they’re finalized. Register and check the studio’s official channels for updates.

Do I need to attend all three days?

Each day has its own theme: the studio and its vision, AI implementation for business, and a product showcase. You can choose the sessions that are most useful to you.

What will I take away?

You’ll get a closer look at EML Tech Studio, practical ideas for thinking about technology and AI in business, and a first look at products and initiatives being showcased.
